# The GC100 lens profile

`profiles/Samsung EK-GC100.lcp` is an Adobe lens profile for the Galaxy Camera EK-GC100's zoom lens (4.1–86.1 mm, f/2.8–5.9). It is generated by `tools/gc100-lcp.py`.

## What it corrects

The camera's RAW frame is the sensor's whole image, before the camera's own processing. At the wide end it shows the lens as it really is: strong barrel distortion, dark corners where the image circle ends, and corner shading. The camera's JPEGs hide all of that: the image processor straightens and crops them.

The profile does the same for RAW (DNG) files in Lightroom, Camera Raw and RawTherapee:

- **Distortion**: the barrel distortion at the wide end, per focal length. It is strongest at 4.1 mm and fades out by about 16.5 mm. From 16.5 mm on, the profile does practically nothing, as the camera does.
- **Vignetting**: an even brightening towards the corners (about +0.7 EV in the corners), the same at every focal length.
- **Not chromatic aberration**: the profile has no CA model. Use Lightroom's "Remove Chromatic Aberration" checkbox, or RawTherapee's CA correction, separately.

The JPEGs from the camera are already corrected. **Don't apply the profile to them.** The profile is marked as a raw profile, so Lightroom offers it for raw files only.

## How to install and use it

### Lightroom Classic, Lightroom (desktop), Camera Raw

1. Copy `Samsung EK-GC100.lcp` to:
   - Windows: `%APPDATA%\Adobe\CameraRaw\LensProfiles\1.0\`
   - macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CameraRaw/LensProfiles/1.0/`
2. Restart the application.
3. **DNGs from GC Camera** already ask for it: their XMP turns on "Enable Profile Corrections" and sets a crop that matches the camera's JPEG framing. Both can be switched off; the pixels in the DNG are untouched.
4. **Other DNGs from this camera**: Develop (or Camera Raw) → Lens Corrections → Profile → tick "Enable Profile Corrections", then pick Make "Samsung", the "Samsung 4.1-86.1mm f/2.8-5.9" lens and the "Samsung EK-GC100" profile if they are not picked automatically.
5. The Distortion and Vignetting sliders under the profile scale its effect (100 = as measured).

Lightroom matches the profile by the DNG's Make (`Samsung`) and Model (`EK-GC100`). DNGs from GC Camera and from `tools/m9mo-dng.py` use exactly those.

### RawTherapee

1. Open the DNG, go to the **Transform** tab, and open **Profiled Lens Correction**.
2. Choose **LCP file** and pick `Samsung EK-GC100.lcp`.
3. Leave "Geometric distortion" and "Vignetting" on.
4. At the wide end, crop the dark corners by hand (the Crop tool). RawTherapee does not read the Lightroom crop stored in the DNG.

RawTherapee picks the right entry from the DNG's focal length. Checked by the user: Lightroom and RawTherapee both land within about 1–2 px of the camera JPEG's geometry.

### darktable and others

darktable uses lensfun, not Adobe profiles, so it cannot use this file. A lensfun profile is planned ([TODO.md](TODO.md), "Offline development").

## How it was made

1. **15 RAW + JPEG pairs** across the zoom range, shot on 2026-11-02. For each shot the camera saved its RAW frame and its own JPEG.
2. **Registration.** Each JPEG was matched to its RAW with SIFT feature points, fitting a radial model. At the centre the JPEG is 1:1 with the RAW, not rotated, and centred at about (0.499, 0.504) of the active area.
3. **The fit.** Adobe's camera model: a corrected point comes from the image at `r × (1 + k1·r² + k2·r⁴)`, with `r` normalised by the half diagonal of the camera JPEG's frame (2880 RAW pixels). The measured values:

   | Focal length | k1 | k2 | JPEG frame within the RAW |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 4.1 mm | −0.269 | +0.117 | about 84% x 85% |
   | 5.0 mm | −0.177 | +0.050 | |
   | 6.2 mm | −0.090 | +0.008 | |
   | 9.2 mm | −0.025 | +0.010 | |
   | 16.5 mm and up | none | none | the whole frame |

   The same table is in `camera-app/gccore/src/lens.cpp`, which the app uses for its own development and for the DNG crop.
4. **Vignetting** was not measured. The value (`VignetteModelParam1 = −0.375`) was set by eye in Lightroom by the author, on real photos.
5. **The file.** `tools/gc100-lcp.py` writes the profile in exactly the shape of Adobe's own `.lcp` files (CRLF, ASCII, attributes on `rdf:Description`). Lightroom finds but refuses other valid XMP shapes, and refuses a whole profile if any entry is all zeros, so the 16.5 mm and 86.1 mm entries carry a negligible k1 of −0.0001 (under 0.03 px in the corners).

The profile is defined for the DNG's default crop, 4654 x 3436 px (8 px inside the 4670 x 3452 active area), with the optical centre at (2331, 1739) in the active area.

To regenerate it: `python3 tools/gc100-lcp.py "profiles/Samsung EK-GC100.lcp"`.

## Limits

- **It reproduces the camera's correction, not a lab calibration.** The reference was the camera's own JPEGs, so whatever distortion the camera leaves in its JPEGs, the profile leaves too. In particular, from 16.5 mm on the camera corrects nothing and neither does the profile.
- **Not every zoom step was measured.** 7.5, 11.3 and 13.9 mm were not shot; Lightroom interpolates between the measured entries there.
- **Corners at the wide end.** The wide-end fits only had points out to about 75% of the radius, so k2 (which dominates in the far corners) is the least certain value.
- **Vignetting is a guess by eye**, a single value for all focal lengths and apertures. A proper measurement needs flat-field shots (a white diffuser over the lens) at every zoom step.
- **One focus distance.** All entries are for focus at infinity; close-up shots may show small differences.
- **The centre is fixed** at the value measured on one camera. Another unit's lens may sit a few pixels off.
- **One camera.** Everything was measured on a single EK-GC100. Copy-to-copy variation is unknown.
