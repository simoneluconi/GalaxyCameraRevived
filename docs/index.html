Android 14 for the Samsung Galaxy Camera

A lightweight LineageOS 21 build for the Galaxy Camera and Galaxy Camera 2 that keeps what makes them cameras: the zoom lever, the shutter button, manual exposure, optical stabilisation and the pop-up flash. And it adds RAW photos.

Built for photography

The zoom lever zooms

Slow and fast stages drive the 21x optical zoom, as on the stock firmware.

A real shutter button

Half press to focus, full press to shoot. Tap the screen to pick the focus point.

P, A, S and M

Set aperture, shutter speed and ISO yourself, with an exposure meter in M.

RAW (DNG)

The sensor's own data next to every JPEG, for Lightroom, Camera Raw and RawTherapee. A lens profile is included.

OIS and the pop-up flash

Optical stabilisation on or off. Raise the flash to use it, close it to turn it off.

Camera-first and light

No Google services and no phone apps: only what a camera needs, with its own camera and gallery apps.

Install

  1. Download Samsung's official firmware for your camera and keep it: it is your way back.
  2. Flash the mini recovery for your model with Odin, and back up the camera's own partitions.
  3. Flash the system for your model with Odin, then do a factory reset from the mini recovery.
  4. The first boot takes a few minutes. Insert a microSD card and start shooting.
Read the install guide from start to end before you flash anything. Never flash a PIT file or a bootloader. Flashing a custom system always has some risk: you do it at your own risk.

Questions

Which models are supported?

The Galaxy Camera EK-GC100 and the Galaxy Camera 2 EK-GC200. Each has its own files: never flash one model's files on the other. The GC110 and GC120 are not supported.

Can I go back to Samsung's firmware?

Yes, with Odin, at any time. The install guide explains how.

Does it have Google Play?

No. There are no Google services and no app store; other apps install as APK files.

How do I update?

Flash the new system package for your model with Odin. Photos, apps and settings are kept.

Where do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub, with your model and the steps that lead to it.
